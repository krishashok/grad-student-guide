Masala Body
Frozen and canned
  1. Freezing stops microbes multiplying while the food is frozen. It slows spoilage. It does not sterilise.
  2. Protein and fibre largely remain. Many vitamins hold up well.
  3. Canning uses heat in a sealed container. Some vitamins are lost. The beans stay nutritious.
  4. Fresh spinach you keep throwing away is worth less than frozen spinach you eat.

How to store and reheat your food

Fridge 4°C / 40°F · Freezer −18°C / 0°F

  1. 1Cool fast in shallow containers. Fridge within 2 hours, 1 hour above 32°C / 90°F.
  2. 2Fridge only what you will eat in 1–2 days. Freeze the rest promptly.
  3. 3Rice: cool within 1 hour, eat within 24 hours or freeze. Reheat once.
  4. 4Thaw in the fridge, or microwave and heat immediately. Reheat to 74°C / 165°F and stir.
